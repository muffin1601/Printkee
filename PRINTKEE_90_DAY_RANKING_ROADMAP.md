# Printkee 90-Day Organic Search Roadmap

Start point: 9 October 2026  
Primary objective: qualified organic visibility and enquiries in India. Rankings are monitored outcomes, not guarantees.

## Days 1–30: protect, measure, and improve proven pages

### Deployment and validation

1. Review and deploy the tested frontend/backend changes together.
2. Verify `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/sitemap`, representative catalog pages, one location page, one blog, and a 404 in production.
3. Submit/confirm `https://printkee.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console.
4. Validate GA4 `view_item`, `select_item`, `search`, `contact`, and `generate_lead` in DebugView without personal information.
5. Export current GSC data by query, page, country, and device for the prior 28 and 90 days.

### P0/P1 content work

Prioritise existing URLs with verified impression/position evidence:

- `/office-and-writing/file-and-folder`
- `/apparel-and-accessories/aprons`
- `/office-and-writing/lanyard-and-id-card`
- `/collection/welcome-kits`
- `/apparel-and-accessories/ties`
- `/bags-and-travel/tote-bags`
- `/apparel-and-accessories/winter-wear`
- `/technology-accessories/wireless-charging`
- `/bags-and-travel/duffle-bags`
- `/` for branded merchandise/corporate merchandise intent

For each, confirm actual materials, available branding methods, artwork inputs, quantity handling, sample policy, price drivers, production/delivery constraints, and relevant FAQs. Do not publish unverified values.

### Technical/operational work

- Review the 19 inventory records absent from the current sitemap crawl and identify inactive seed records versus missing active database records.
- Export the current SEO opportunity CSV from admin, retain a backup, and test dry-run validation with a small non-production sample before any import.
- Add a release annotation and freeze title/H1 changes on recently optimised pages for a comparable 28-day window unless a defect appears.

### Day-30 measures

- Index coverage and sitemap discovered/indexed counts.
- Organic impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, and leads for the prioritised pages.
- Query-to-page ownership and cannibalisation.
- GA4 event receipt and lead funnel completeness.
- Mobile CWV status by template.

## Days 31–60: build topical support without page inflation

### Editorial briefs

Prepare and human-review briefs for:

1. How to plan employee welcome kits: recipient data, product mix, branding, packaging, and delivery.
2. Custom T-shirt printing methods: screen print, embroidery, transfer, and method-selection criteria using verified capabilities only.
3. Corporate merchandise budget planning without publishing fabricated prices.
4. Artwork preparation for logo branding on apparel, bags, drinkware, stationery, and tech products.
5. Event/exhibition merchandise planning by attendee volume, distribution method, and required-in-hand date.
6. Sustainable promotional products: how to verify material claims and avoid vague green claims.
7. Corporate Diwali gifting preparation timeline for the next relevant season.

Each brief needs a distinct intent, an expert reviewer, publication/review dates, at least two relevant commercial links, a quotation path, and only visible schema.

### Internal links

- Add contextual links from approved guides to the best matching canonical category/subcategory.
- Add relevant guide links back from major category pages where they help selection.
- Persist page-level inbound/outbound counts in the crawl report and flag important pages deeper than four clicks or with fewer than three useful inbound links.
- Avoid keyword-heavy sitewide footer expansion.

### Location validation

- Evaluate Mumbai, Pune, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, and other researched cities using GSC demand, sales/service capability, fulfilment constraints, and unique content availability.
- Keep non-approved locations as research records. Do not publish city-name substitution pages.
- Review the six existing Delhi NCR pages for conversion and query fit; consolidate any page that cannot sustain unique value.

### Day-60 measures

- Positions 4–10 and 11–20 by page/query.
- High-impression/low-CTR pages and snippet changes.
- Organic-assisted `generate_lead` volume and rate.
- Internal-link coverage and orphan count.
- Indexed versus submitted pages by page type.

## Days 61–90: validate expansion and compound authority

### Product/service expansion decisions

The 780 expansion-keyword rows cover printing, labels/stickers, packaging, business print, photo products, decor, and related needs. Business owners must decide which offerings are genuinely available. Only then should the team:

1. Define the product/service scope and fulfilment process.
2. Add real products or service records.
3. Create one canonical page per distinct need.
4. Supply original useful content and internal links.
5. Pass the existing quality gate before indexation.

### International research

For UAE, US, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, and other markets, verify fulfilment, delivery economics, currency, legal/tax implications, customer support, local intent, and language. Do not create country pages or hreflang until real localized equivalents exist.

### Authority and trust

- Audit Printkee profiles on legitimate marketplaces/directories for consistent name, address, phone, categories, and URLs.
- Seek permission-backed supplier/client case evidence and relevant industry mentions.
- Do not buy bulk links, syndicate exact-match pages, or publish unsupported awards/testimonials.

### Day-90 review

- Compare equivalent 28-day and 90-day periods, segmented by country/device.
- Identify winners, declines, cannibalisation, no-impression indexable pages, and pages ranking 4–20.
- Refresh successful pages based on query evidence.
- Merge/noindex pages that fail differentiation rather than multiplying variants.
- Approve the next small batch only when product fit, unique content, internal links, and measurement ownership are clear.

## Governance cadence

- Weekly: sitemap/indexation errors, backend availability, 404s, and lead-capture health.
- Monthly: GSC query/page/country/device import, CTR and position cohorts, GA4 lead events, and content change log.
- Quarterly: product facts, business details, location capability, structured data, internal-link graph, stale content, and competitor landscape.

Success is improved relevant impressions, qualified clicks, and enquiries on supported offerings—not the number of pages published.
