# Printkee Authority and Backlink Plan

## Objective

Build legitimate evidence and references around Printkee's priority commercial clusters: corporate gifting, branded merchandise, employee welcome kits, custom apparel, office merchandise, bags, technology gifts, sustainable products and seasonal gifting.

Authority work should support useful commercial pages. It must not rely on purchased links, private blog networks, mass guest posts, link farms, automated submissions or irrelevant directories.

## 1. Business and partner citations

- Ask verified supplier and brand partners whether Printkee can be listed as an authorised business-gifting or bulk-order partner.
- Maintain consistent approved business name, phone, email and address across Google Business Profile and reputable Indian business directories.
- Seek profiles in legitimate gifting, printing, promotional-products, HR/procurement and event-industry associations where membership is real.
- Create accurate vendor profiles for procurement platforms that buyers already use; avoid thin directories created only for links.

Evidence required: contractual/partner status, approved brand usage, canonical business details, and destination URL.

## 2. Customer-led authority

- Request permission to publish real project case studies.
- Use a consistent format: requirement, recipient group, product selection, verified branding method, quantity, logistics challenge and measurable outcome where the customer approves it.
- Ask participating customers to link from a supplier, partner, newsroom or event recap page when editorially appropriate.
- Collect attributable testimonials only with written permission; never create anonymous proof to imitate customer evidence.

Priority case-study themes: employee welcome kits, event merchandise, branded apparel, multi-location fulfilment, and seasonal corporate gifting.

## 3. Event and community partnerships

- Provide merchandise for genuine conferences, trade associations, campus programmes or business events where Printkee is a named partner.
- Request inclusion on the official sponsor/partner page and event recap, using a branded or company anchor rather than repeated exact-match keywords.
- Build conference-merchandise checklists with event organisers using original lessons from completed projects.

## 4. Original procurement resources

Publish resources only after an experienced team member validates them:

- Corporate gifting requirement template
- Employee welcome-kit brief and checklist
- Artwork handoff checklist for print and embroidery
- Branding-method comparison using verified production constraints
- Multi-location recipient-data and dispatch checklist
- Seasonal gifting planning calendar
- Anonymised aggregate research derived from real quotation data, only where privacy and commercial sensitivity permit

Each resource should link naturally to one primary commercial page and be useful enough for HR, procurement, admin or event teams to reference independently.

## 5. Digital PR

- Pitch original, sourced observations rather than promotional listicles.
- Potential themes: changes in onboarding-kit composition, procurement lead-time planning, common artwork errors, or anonymised seasonal demand patterns.
- Target Indian HR, workplace, procurement, startup, events, marketing and sustainability publications with genuinely relevant data.
- Keep a source pack containing methodology, sample size, limitations and an accountable spokesperson.

## 6. Sustainable-gifting claims

Do not seek coverage around “eco-friendly” products until material composition, certifications and supplier documentation are available. Prefer precise claims about a verified product over broad sustainability language.

## 7. 90-day outreach sequence

### Days 1–30

- Confirm business identity and compile partner/customer permission lists.
- Reconcile Google Business Profile and core citations.
- Select two completed projects suitable for case studies.
- Draft the procurement requirement template and artwork checklist.

### Days 31–60

- Publish one approved case study and one original procurement resource.
- Contact relevant suppliers, associations and event partners.
- Build a prospect list based on topical fit and editorial quality, not domain metrics alone.

### Days 61–90

- Publish the second case study.
- Pitch one data-supported story to a small set of relevant publications.
- Review earned referring pages, branded searches, assisted conversions and GSC movement for linked destinations.

## Measurement

- New relevant referring domains and linked pages
- Links to priority commercial URLs versus only the homepage
- Referral visits and qualified enquiries
- Growth in branded queries
- GSC impressions, CTR and position for supported clusters
- Link retention and topical relevance after 30/60/90 days

Authority acquisition improves competitive potential but does not guarantee top-ten rankings.
