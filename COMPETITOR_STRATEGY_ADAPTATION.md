# Purple Palette Strategy Adaptation for Printkee

Source reviewed: all 28 pages of `PurplePalette.in SEO Reverse-Engineering Report (1).pdf`.  
Evidence boundary: the report itself sampled eight Purple Palette pages, mapped roughly 245 URLs from its HTML hubs, and explicitly lacked verified rankings, full crawl data, Search Console, backlink metrics, Core Web Vitals, and retrievable XML-sitemap/robots evidence.

No Purple Palette copy, branding, descriptions, or templates are reproduced.

| Competitor finding | Classification | Printkee adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Clear category to subcategory architecture | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Retain Printkee's database-led category/subcategory/product hierarchy and descriptive nested URLs. Avoid Purple Palette's flat taxonomy and inconsistent slug conventions. |
| Modifier-by-product keyword matrix | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Use it for research and clustering. Publish one canonical page per distinct buyer need, not one page per modifier. |
| Commercial supplier/manufacturer/wholesale vocabulary | REQUIRES FURTHER EVIDENCE | Use neutral sourcing and bulk-order language. Do not call Printkee a manufacturer, factory, wholesaler, or distributor without owner evidence. |
| Geographic targeting | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Keep the six differentiated Delhi NCR service-area pages. Research other cities, but require service feasibility and distinct content before publication. |
| One primary keyword and supporting terms per URL | ADOPT | Enforce through the existing normalized-primary-keyword uniqueness constraint and the master keyword map. |
| Short descriptive slugs | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Preserve existing descriptive nested slugs and one canonical trailing-slash convention. Do not migrate established catalog URLs for cosmetic reasons. |
| Category-to-subcategory cards and links | ADOPT | Continue database-driven taxonomy navigation and related product/category links. |
| HTML sitemap hub | ADOPT | Retain and expand `/sitemap` from approved canonical routes only. |
| Breadcrumb navigation | ADOPT | Continue visible breadcrumbs plus BreadcrumbList schema on matching pages. |
| Quote/enquiry CTA on commercial pages | ADOPT | Preserve requirement-led quote flows and fire `generate_lead` only after successful capture. |
| Seasonal gifting pages | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Maintain reviewed Diwali pages and update them ahead of demand. Create other seasonal pages only with real products and timely editorial review. |
| B2B facts, client logos, and trust signals | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Show only verified business/contact details, capabilities, examples, and permissions-backed customer evidence. Avoid self-asserted awards, satisfaction rates, and unverifiable superlatives. |
| Directory and marketplace visibility | REQUIRES FURTHER EVIDENCE | Audit NAP and brand accuracy first. Pursue legitimate profiles where Printkee has a genuine business relationship; do not treat listing presence as ranking proof. |
| Blog-to-commercial internal links | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Each new or refreshed guide should link contextually to the best matching category/product and quotation path. Avoid generic sitewide exact-match blocks. |
| Product/use-case/recipient/occasion cluster breadth | ADOPT WITH IMPROVEMENTS | Use the supplied universe to identify real clusters, then gate publication on product fit, unique content, and buyer value. |
| Location pages cloned from the homepage | REJECT | This is doorway/scaled-content risk. No city-name substitution templates. |
| Thin 120–160 word commercial pages | REJECT | Content depth should be driven by verified specifications, selection guidance, artwork needs, ordering steps, delivery constraints, and useful FAQs—not an arbitrary word count. |
| Multiple near-duplicate mug/sipper/bag/Diwali pages | REJECT | Consolidate synonyms and close intent variants on one canonical URL; use redirects only for genuine legacy migrations. |
| Long brand-free keyword-stacked titles | REJECT | Use distinct, readable titles with Printkee branding where appropriate. |
| Meta keywords and obsolete tags | REJECT | They provide no Google ranking value and add noise. |
| Keyword-stuffed image alt/title text | REJECT | Alt text should describe the actual image; do not inject locations or supplier terms. |
| Product URLs that resolve/canonicalise to category pages | REJECT | Preserve unique, valid Printkee product URLs and return true 404s for unknown products. |
| Sitewide country selector embedded in every quote form | REJECT | Keep the enquiry interface focused on markets that can actually be served. |
| LocalBusiness schema repeated on location pages | REJECT | Use Organization globally. Add LocalBusiness only for a verified physical business location represented on the page. |
| Index/follow on every generated page | REJECT | Draft, duplicate, archived, or unreviewed records remain noindex and outside the sitemap. |

## Recommended Printkee model

1. Treat the catalog hierarchy as the primary commercial architecture.
2. Map all variants—custom/customised, personalised/personalized, supplier/vendor, singular/plural, T-shirt spellings, and Delhi NCR geography—to consolidated canonical clusters.
3. Use the managed SEO-page engine only for needs the catalog cannot satisfy and only after quality review.
4. Use `/corporate-gifting`, `/industries`, `/use-cases`, `/locations`, `/blogs`, and `/sitemap` as controlled discovery hubs.
5. Strengthen existing pages with verified product details, artwork requirements, selection criteria, quote inputs, FAQs, and contextual links.
6. Import current Search Console evidence regularly and measure by query, page, country, and device before expanding page counts.
7. Keep India primary. International keyword rows are research records until service, delivery, currency, and localisation are confirmed.

## Evidence still required

- Fresh GSC query-by-page export and URL Inspection/index-coverage data.
- Current GA4 event validation and lead attribution.
- Product-level MOQ, branding methods, lead times, materials, dimensions, and delivery constraints from business owners/suppliers.
- Verified offices/service capabilities outside the current New Delhi business address.
- International fulfilment and market requirements.
- Consistent NAP and business facts across third-party profiles.
- Paid keyword demand/difficulty data if the business wants volume-led prioritisation.

The competitor's useful lesson is disciplined keyword-to-page ownership and crawl paths. Its unsafe lesson is scale without enough differentiation. Printkee should adopt the former and explicitly reject the latter.
