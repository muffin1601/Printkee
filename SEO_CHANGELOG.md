## Measurement protocol

1. Annotate the production deployment date in GA4 and the team reporting calendar.
2. Do not make another title/H1 change on these URLs for at least 28 days unless a factual or technical defect appears.
3. Compare equivalent 28-day periods by query and page: clicks, impressions, CTR and position.
4. Segment India and device type; do not interpret average position without the associated query mix.
5. Use a combined query-by-page Search Console export for the next review to confirm which URL Google actually selected for each cluster.
6. Record every later edit in this file before deployment.

No change listed here guarantees a ranking, traffic or revenue outcome.
