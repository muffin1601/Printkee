# Programmatic SEO Research

`PRINTKEE_GENERATED_URL_CANDIDATES.csv` contains 14,638 research candidates. Generation does not mean publication. Candidates, canonicalized rows, and rejected rows stay outside the sitemap unless a separate landing page passes quality review and becomes indexable.

Use the approved-indexable, conflict, rejected/merged, and product-location reports to review route ownership before creating any database-backed page.
