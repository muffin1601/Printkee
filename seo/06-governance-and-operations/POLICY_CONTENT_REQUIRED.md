# Policy content requiring legal/owner approval

The Privacy Policy is published at `/privacy-policy` and describes the implemented data flows, but it still requires professional legal review against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

The following pages must not be published from generic template text. Obtain approved business terms for:

1. Terms of Service: contracting entity, quotation acceptance, payment, artwork approval, intellectual property, prohibited artwork, warranties, liability, force majeure, governing law and dispute process.
2. Shipping and Delivery: service areas, dispatch versus delivery definitions, address changes, split shipments, courier risk, delays, failed delivery and damage reporting.
3. Returns, Refunds and Cancellation: customised-goods eligibility, defects, evidence windows, replacements, cancellations after artwork/production approval, refund timing and exclusions.
4. Cookie/Tracking notice: consent requirements and controls for GA4 and Meta Pixel, including whether a consent-management platform is required.
5. Privacy operations: lawful grounds/notices, retention schedule, data-principal request workflow, grievance contact, processor list and cross-border transfers.

Until approved content exists, the footer and HTML sitemap should link only to the real Privacy Policy rather than routing policy labels to the contact page.
